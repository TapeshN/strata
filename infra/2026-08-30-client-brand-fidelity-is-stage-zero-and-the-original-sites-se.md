reface pipeline now runs extraction FIRST (logo files, pixel-sampled exact hexes, full section inventory with keep/drop judgments, embed mechanics) and the verify stage grades against the CLIENT'S site inventory; one-home rule per asset (book, documentary) enforced by grep counts in verify.

for any client with existing infra — brand tokens come from their assets (color must match the logo), section order in their current site is read as their priority ranking, and every existing feature needs an explicit keep/drop decision in the build contract.
