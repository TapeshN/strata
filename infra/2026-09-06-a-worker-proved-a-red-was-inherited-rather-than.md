blocking on an inherited red teaches every future worker not to run the honest gate — the first session to look carefully inherits the whole tree's debt. Separate *verified pre-existing* from *root cause identified* and never collapse them; the first is a merge decision, the second is a brief.

the cheapest way to keep deep verification happening is to make sure it never punishes the person who does it.
