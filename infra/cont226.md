a CORRECTION IS A CLAIM and carries the same evidence bar as the thing it corrects. Before overturning a hypothesis, state what the new evidence actually rules out — "this record proves at least one SMS arrived" is not "delivery is healthy". Before acting on a constant, read what the file says the constant BOUNDS; a TTL and a staleness window are different numbers with different names sitting ten lines apart.

the shape to distrust is a fast reversal that feels like rigour. Both of mine were reversals of a correct instinct, made confidently, on evidence that answered a narrower question than the one I was asking.
