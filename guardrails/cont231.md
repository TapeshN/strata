the distinction is whether the PROPERTY survives. A legitimate update restates the same property against the new shape and, where possible, strengthens it — here the assertion still pins "threaded, not siblings" and gained a second assertion that the slot is exactly those two controls. An illegitimate one loosens the predicate until it stops discriminating.

state the property in words before touching the assertion, and check the new assertion still fails when the property is violated. If you cannot say what would now break it, you deleted a guard rather than updating one. Every reversed policy test must also cite the ruling that authorised the reversal — three reviewers caught that mine cited a DECISIONS entry I had never written.
