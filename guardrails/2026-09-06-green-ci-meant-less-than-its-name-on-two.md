a receipt states what it actually witnessed. Where a gate cannot be independent by construction, say so in the artifact rather than in a reviewer's head.

when a mechanism's name contains a strong word — independent, verified, complete — check the word against the mechanism, not against the intent. Both defects here were true statements about what someone wanted and false statements about what ran.
