when a brief builds the mechanism an invariant constrains, the brief states the carve-out and the invariant carries the scope clause, written at authoring time rather than discovered by a blocked worker. Bases are derived from the stacking rule mechanically, never typed per brief.

rules written as absolutes need their ACTOR and their PLACE stated — "no names" meant no names in the repository, not in a runtime message; "two reviews before any merge" meant merges of code. An absolute rule with an unstated scope will collide with the first mechanism that has to operate inside it.

a fleet that stops on contradictions is worth more than one that resolves them silently — each stop cost minutes and bought a corrected contract; the alternative was fourteen workers drifting in fourteen directions.
